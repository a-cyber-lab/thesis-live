How to read this

Every 10 minutes the controller plans the next 12 hours using only what was published before the decision: AEMO pre-dispatch price forecasts and Open-Meteo solar forecasts. Each step is then settled at the actual 5-minute dispatch prices.

Share kept compares that live saving with a perfect-foresight run over the same steps, which knows the actual prices and solar in advance. Perfect foresight is the usual assumption in battery studies; the gap is the cost of real forecasts.

This is a shadow plant: the solar output is Open-Meteo's estimate and the household load is modelled, so it tests the controller and its data pipeline in real time rather than measuring a real bill.
